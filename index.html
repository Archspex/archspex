Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about ArchSpex, product discovery, brand listings and platform memberships.

General

What is ArchSpex?+−
ArchSpex is a curated specification platform connecting architects, designers, developers, contractors and manufacturers across GCC projects.
Is ArchSpex free to use?+−
Yes. Product discovery, product research and specification resources are available free of charge for construction professionals.
Which countries does ArchSpex cover?+−
ArchSpex focuses on GCC projects, including the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman, with selected visibility across the wider Middle East.
Are all products verified?+−
All manufacturers and product listings are reviewed before publication to maintain platform quality and relevance.

For Professionals

Who can use ArchSpex?+−
Architects, designers, developers, contractors, procurement teams and other construction professionals.
Can I download BIM and CAD files?+−
Yes. Manufacturers may upload BIM models, CAD drawings and technical resources where available.
Can I request product samples?+−
Yes. Sample requests can be submitted directly through the platform.
Can I submit RFQs?+−
Yes. Professionals can request quotations from manufacturers through ArchSpex.
Does ArchSpex sell products directly?+−
No. ArchSpex connects professionals with manufacturers and suppliers but does not sell products directly.

For Brands

Who can list products on ArchSpex?+−
Manufacturers, distributors and product brands relevant to GCC construction and design projects.
Is every brand accepted?+−
No. Applications are reviewed to ensure product relevance, quality and suitability for the platform.
What information can be included in a listing?+−
Brand profiles, product information, technical documents, BIM files, certifications, images and project references.
How long does the review process take?+−
Most applications are reviewed within 2 business days.

Memberships & Listings

What membership plans are available?+−
ArchSpex offers Essential, Professional and Enterprise membership levels designed to support different visibility and specification objectives.
Are membership prices published online?+−
Membership plans are discussed directly with manufacturers to ensure the most suitable package and visibility strategy.
Can I upgrade my membership later?+−
Yes. Brands may upgrade their membership level at any time.
What visibility opportunities are available?+−
Options include featured listings, homepage placements, category sponsorships and newsletter features.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the approval process take?+
Applications are typically reviewed within one business day. Our team will contact you to arrange a short consultation before your profile goes live.
Can we upgrade or downgrade our membership?+
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your membership at any time to match your business goals.
Can ArchSpex help us enter the GCC market?+
Absolutely. Our platform is designed specifically to help international manufacturers gain visibility and enter the GCC market efficiently.
Can distributors or representatives apply?+
Yes. Distributors and authorised representatives are welcome. We will discuss the best structure for your listing during the consultation.
Who uploads our products and content?+
You control what appears on your profile. Our team can also assist with onboarding, uploads and content setup during your first month.
Is there a contract or minimum commitment?+
Memberships are typically annual. Full details are shared during the consultation so you can choose the option that suits you.
