FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about Archspex, product discovery, brand listings and platform memberships.

General

What is Archspex?+−
Archspex is a specification platform that connects the architecture, design, and construction community in the UAE & GCC with verified international building material brands.
Is Archspex free to use?+−
Yes. Browsing products, downloading BIM/CAD files, requesting samples, and submitting RFQs is free for architects, designers, contractors, and project developers.
Which countries does Archspex cover?+−
Archspex focuses on the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and the wider GCC region, with growing reach across the Middle East.
Are all products verified?+−
Yes. Every brand and product listed on Archspex goes through an internal review before going live on the platform.

For Professionals

Who can use Archspex?+−
Architects, interior designers, contractors, project developers, and procurement teams working on projects across the GCC.
Can I download BIM and CAD files?+−
Yes — where brands have provided them, BIM, CAD, and technical files are available directly from each product page.
Can I request product samples?+−
Yes. You can request samples directly from manufacturers through the product page.
Can I submit RFQs?+−
Yes. You can send Requests for Quotation directly to listed brands through the product or brand profile.
Does Archspex sell products directly?+−
No. Archspex is a specification platform — all transactions and supply happen directly between the project team and the manufacturer or distributor.

For Brands

Who can list products on Archspex?+−
Manufacturers and authorised distributors of building materials and architectural products serving the GCC region.
Is every brand accepted?+−
No. Archspex is a curated platform — brands are reviewed for product quality, project relevance, and documentation before being approved.
What information can be included in a listing?+−
Product descriptions, technical data sheets, BIM/CAD files, certifications, sample availability, and project references.
How long does the review process take?+−
Brand applications are typically reviewed within 2 business days.

Memberships & Listings

What membership plans are available?+−
Archspex offers Standard, Featured, and Premium plans — each unlocking additional listing capacity, profile visibility, and project tools.
Are membership prices published online?+−
Detailed pricing is shared after your brand application is approved, so plans can be matched to your product portfolio.
Can I upgrade my membership later?+−
Yes. Brands can upgrade at any time as their visibility and listing needs grow.
What visibility opportunities are available?+−
Beyond standard listings, brands can access Featured Placements, Homepage Visibility, Category Sponsorship, and Newsletter Features.
