About Archspex

The specification platform built for the Gulf

Archspex connects UAE & GCC construction professionals with verified building material manufacturers — making specification faster, smarter and more reliable.

Our Mission

Simplifying how professionals specify materials

The UAE & GCC construction market is one of the most active in the world — yet finding the right building materials still means chasing catalogues, waiting on emails and managing fragmented supplier relationships.

Archspex was built to change that. One platform where architects, contractors and developers can find specification-ready products, download BIM files, request samples and get quotes — without leaving their desk.
